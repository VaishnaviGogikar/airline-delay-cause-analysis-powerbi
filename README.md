# ✈️ Airline Delay Cause Analysis | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing U.S. airline delays and operational performance from 2013–2023.

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze airline operational data and derive business-focused insights into carrier performance, airport risk, delay trends, cancellations, and delay causes.

## 📌 Key KPIs

- ✈️ Arriving Flights: 62.1M
- ⏱️ Delay Rate: 18.3%
- 🕐 Total Delay Minutes: 726.8M
- ⏱️ Average Delay per Delayed Flight: 63.9 min

## 🧹 Data Preparation

- Cleaned and validated approximately 171K records using Power Query
- Investigated and handled missing values
- Validated the Year–Month–Carrier–Airport data grain
- Investigated carrier naming inconsistencies
- Standardized reporting names while preserving source data

## 🧠 Data Modeling & DAX

- Created a dedicated Date dimension
- Established an active Date relationship
- Developed 19 DAX measures for delay rates, cancellation rates, YoY analysis, carrier ranking, delay causes, and operational impact

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 🔵 Executive Overview
Overall airline performance, KPIs, flight volume, delay trends, monthly delay rates, cancellations, and major delay causes.

### 🟢 Carrier Analysis
Carrier reliability, delay-rate trends, cancellation performance, and late-aircraft delay impact.

### 🟣 Airport & Delay Cause Analysis
Airport delay risk, operational impact, and delay-cause contribution across major airports.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Late-aircraft delays were the largest contributor among the analyzed delay causes.
- Delay and cancellation patterns changed considerably across 2013–2023.
- Delay rate and cancellation rate highlight different dimensions of operational reliability.
- High-volume airports were used for focused airport risk analysis to avoid unstable rates from low-volume airports.
